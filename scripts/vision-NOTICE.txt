Optional photo suggestions (on-device image understanding)

Model: TinyCLIP ViT-40M/32 (image tower, 8-bit weights), https://github.com/microsoft/Cream/tree/main/TinyCLIP,
weights https://huggingface.co/wkcn/TinyCLIP-ViT-40M-32-Text-19M-LAION400M, MIT License. Trained by distillation on
LAION-400M. The ONNX export onnx-community/TinyCLIP-ViT-40M-32-Text-19M-LAION400M-ONNX is an automatic conversion of
those MIT weights and states no licence of its own; this file keeps only its image tower and quantizes its weights
(frontend/scripts/vision-model.py).

Runtime: ONNX Runtime Web (onnxruntime-web, the WebAssembly build ort-wasm-simd-threaded.wasm),
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime, MIT License. The npm package ships no licence or third-party notices; the
runtime's ThirdPartyNotices (for code compiled into the WebAssembly build, such as Eigen under MPL-2.0) are taken from
the upstream repository at the pinned version and are part of the app's THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt.
